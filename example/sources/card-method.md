# The Card Method

## Summary

A short guide to drafting from a deck of cards: one idea per card,
shuffled until the order of the argument becomes clear.

> Put one idea on each card. If you need a second sentence to explain
> the first, you need a second card.

> Sorting the deck is the outline. You don't write an outline first;
> you discover it on the table.

## Practical notes

- 3×5 inches is enough room for a quote and where it came from
- Always write the source on the card itself
